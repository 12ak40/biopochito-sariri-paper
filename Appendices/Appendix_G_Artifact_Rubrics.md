# Appendix G: Artifact Rubrics and Evaluator Guide (Stations B and C)

## Station B (Stories and Art) - 4-point Rubric
1. **Connection to Lake Titicaca:** 1 (None) to 4 (Integrates multiple specific elements).
2. **Creativity:** 1 (Copied) to 4 (Highly original).
3. **Expression:** 1 (Unclear) to 4 (Clear, descriptive, emotive).
4. **Detail (Drawing):** 1 (Minimal) to 4 (Highly detailed, creative use of color).

## Station C (Video) - 4-point Rubric
1. **Scientific Evidence:** 1 (None) to 4 (Multiple correct facts).
2. **Empathy:** 1 (None) to 4 (Deep emotional connection).
3. **Call to Action:** 1 (None) to 4 (Specific, motivating action).
4. **Responsible Communication (MIL):** 1 (Irresponsible) to 4 (Critical thinking, ethical communication).
