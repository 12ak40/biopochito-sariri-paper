# Biopochito Knowledge Database Provenance

The Biopochito AI system relies on a curated offline database of approximately 500 entries. To ensure transparency and avoid copyright infringement, we document the provenance of this knowledge:

| Source Category | Number of Entries | Description / Provenance | Copyright / License Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| JLASC Publications | ~150 | Peer-reviewed articles on Lake Titicaca biodiversity and environmental education. | Paraphrased text. No copyrighted text was copied verbatim. |
| Local Ecological Records | ~250 | Educational materials provided by local school educators (Unidad Educativa Sariri) regarding totora reeds, local fish, and Andean vizcacha. | Educational use permission granted by the school. |
| UNESCO Green MIL Framework | ~100 | Definitions and pedagogical examples of media and information literacy adapted for children. | Public domain / Open access. Paraphrased. |

Review Process: All entries were reviewed by the research team and two local educators to ensure cultural accuracy, age-appropriateness (reading level for ages 8-17), and scientific factuality.
